## D. Ongoing monitoring

The case is developing daily (AG investigation began Oct 1; grand jury process starting).
Re-run the fetchers to pick up anything new:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File D:\Cornell-7\07_Metadata\fetch.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File D:\Cornell-7\07_Metadata\fetch_wayback.ps1 -DelaySeconds 45
```

`fetch.ps1` is idempotent — it skips files whose SHA-256 already matches the ledger and only
fetches genuinely new items. `fetch_wayback.ps1` routes bot-blocked sources through the
Internet Archive; **give it a long `-DelaySeconds` (45+)**, because web.archive.org returns
429 to this host very quickly and that is what stalled the automated pass.

Best recurring targets, in priority order:
1. NYSCEF docket (C1) — new filings land here first
2. `https://ag.ny.gov/press-releases-for-month/YYYYMM` — swap in the current month
3. `https://statements.cornell.edu/` — Cornell's statement archive
4. `https://www.cornellsun.com/` — broke the story and named the seven
5. Governor's office press releases

## Notes on scope

The manifest is deliberately scoped to **public record**: court filings, official statements,
published institutional reports, published journalism, and archived copies of public pages.
It does not attempt to obtain non-public material — Title IX interview transcripts, grand
jury records, or disciplinary files, which are not lawfully public and remain sealed or
in-custody. Some of that material has surfaced in journalists' reporting, and those reports
are captured here as journalism; the underlying records are not.
