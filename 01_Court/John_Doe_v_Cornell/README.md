# John Doe v. Cornell University — public court records

These redacted or publicly filed records concern a separate Tompkins County Article 78
proceeding challenging Cornell's student-conduct decision. The petitioner is identified in
the filings as John Doe. The records became newly accessible through DocumentCloud on
October 3, 2026; Anna Bower's October 5 document roundup links to the collection.

The folder intentionally contains court filings and redacted records only. It does not
rehost private photographs or private-message screenshots. The court's September 23
decision dismissed the petition; an appeal was reported as filed in October 2026. The
campus disciplinary findings described in these records are not criminal convictions.
