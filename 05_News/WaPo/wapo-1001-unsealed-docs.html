Documents detail Cornell investigation into allegations of rape at fraternity house

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul on Thursday said that she had lost faith in the county district attorney’s ability to fairly oversee the case and had appointed state Attorney General Letitia James as special prosecutor.

October 1, 2026 at 11:50 p.m. EDTYesterday at 11:50 p.m. EDT
7 min

By Susan Svrluga
, 
Todd Wallack
and 
Amy B Wang

Cornell University conducted an internal disciplinary investigation involving more than 50 interviews with 32 witnesses after a student reported she was drugged and raped by several men at a fraternity house in October 2024, according to court documents unsealed in New York this week.
