Presidential Task Force on Campus Sexual Assault

In February 2025, President Kotlikoff and Provost Bala established a task force charged with understanding and addressing sexual assault involving undergraduate students on the Ithaca campus.

In March 2026, university leadership endorsed the task force’s final report (PDF) and committed to implementing the recommendations. The recommendations include strategies to target social conditions that enable assault and to foster a sexually safe and healthy campus, rooted in a framework that addresses individual behavior, community culture, and institutional accountability. Among the key measures to be implemented are launching a public health campaign; increasing students’ knowledge and clarity of reporting options and confidential resources; developing new educational opportunities, including courses focused on sexual citizenship; prioritizing evidence based prevention strategies; and expanding training for students, leaders, and residential staff.

The task force co-chairs have formed an implementation committee to put the report’s recommendations into practice with the aspiration to instill a culture where students are protected, empowered, and informed about sexual violence prevention. Share questions and feedback with the task force at tfsa@cornell.edu.
